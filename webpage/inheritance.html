Set up your legacy plan in four simple steps

01

Open your settings

Go to your profile settings in the Nexos Bank app and tap on the Beneficiaries section.

02

Add your loved ones

Enter your beneficiary’s details, including their full name, date of birth, and relationship to you.

03

Allocate your wealth

Choose your distribution by specifying the exact percentage of your holdings you wish each person to receive.

04

Confirm and secure

Save your preferences to receive an immediate confirmation that your legacy plan is active and protected.
